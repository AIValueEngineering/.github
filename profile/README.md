# AIVE · AI Value Engineering

**AI-Native Enterprise Value Systems & Practice**

*Research. Build. Validate. Evolve.*

AIValueEngineering is an independent research and engineering initiative focused on how people, AI systems, business processes, and organizations can work together to create **measurable, sustainable enterprise value**.

Our long-term direction is to develop the knowledge, methods, engineering practice, and evidence needed for responsible AI-native enterprise value architecture.

## Our mission · 我们的使命

研究、构建、实践并持续完善 **AI 原生企业价值体系**：从真实业务问题出发，设计可治理的 AI 系统和人机协作机制，通过工程运行与现实证据检验其价值，并持续修正、复制与演进。

We explore how to design, build, operate, and evaluate AI-native enterprise capabilities while retaining human accountability, sound governance, and independent verification.

## Focus areas · 研究方向

### AI Builder · Open practice

An open learning and building community associated with the book **《AI构建者崛起》 (The Rise of AI Builders)**.

Our practice model follows three connected cycles:

1. **Co-design the blueprint** — define the real problem, value, constraints, permissions, and evidence requirements.
2. **Build and run with AI** — create a working system, workflow, skill, or agent with appropriate human oversight.
3. **Evolve through real feedback** — examine actual results, failures, costs, and evidence before deciding what to change.

The community is being prepared. Open resources and reproducible examples will be added only after provenance and publication review.

### AI-Native Engineering · Applied research

Agents, Skills, workflows, business semantics/ontology, evaluations, traces, permissions, and responsible deployment.

### Enterprise Value Engineering · Professional research

Enterprise value definition, measurable outcomes, business process and organizational design, human-AI responsibility, and reproducible value-evaluation methods.

Professional FDE research is maintained separately from employer- or customer-owned operational materials.

## How we work · 工作原则

- **Evidence before claims:** distinguish a prototype, a deployed workflow, a measurable outcome, and repeated value creation.
- **Human agency and responsibility:** AI assists judgment and execution; people retain legitimate decision rights and accountability.
- **Reproducibility and learning:** publish what can be reproduced and explain known limitations and failures.
- **Respect for rights and confidentiality:** do not publish employer data, customer information, private AI-brain content, or third-party materials without appropriate rights and authorization.
- **Continuous revision:** update models, tools, and methods when better evidence emerges.
