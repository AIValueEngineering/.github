# AIVE | AI Value Engineering

**AI-Native Enterprise Value Systems & Practice**

*Research. Build. Validate. Evolve.*

AIValueEngineering (AIVE) is an independent research and engineering initiative exploring how people, AI systems, business processes, and organizations can work together to create **measurable, sustainable enterprise value**.

Our long-term ambition is to advance AI-native enterprise value architecture through original research, reproducible engineering, and evidence-based practice.

## Our Mission

To research, design, build, and continually improve AI-native enterprise value systems.

We start with real business problems, develop governed AI capabilities and human-AI workflows, test their outcomes in practice, and refine our methods as evidence emerges. Human accountability, sound judgment, and responsible deployment remain essential.

## Focus Areas

### AI Builder | Open Community

An open learning and building community connected to the book project **The Rise of AI Builders** (working English title).

Our practical learning model consists of three connected cycles:

1. **Co-design the blueprint.** Define the problem, intended value, constraints, responsibilities, permissions, and success criteria.
2. **Build and run with AI.** Create a usable agent, skill, workflow, or system; test it under appropriate human oversight.
3. **Evolve through real-world feedback.** Examine outcomes, failures, costs, and evidence; then improve, roll back, or stop as needed.

The community is in preparation. Public examples and starter resources will be released after appropriate review.

### AI-Native Engineering | Applied Research

Exploring agents, skills, workflows, business ontology, evaluations, execution traces, permissions, and reliable deployment.

### FDE & Enterprise Value Engineering | Professional Research

Exploring business outcomes, value measurement, enterprise process design, human-AI operating models, forward-deployed engineering (FDE), and continuous value realization.

Independent professional research is kept separate from employer-owned systems, customer information, and confidential operational work.

## Principles

- **Evidence before claims.** Distinguish demonstrations, deployed systems, measured business outcomes, and repeatable results.
- **Human accountability.** AI can support decisions and execution, while people retain legitimate responsibility and oversight.
- **Reproducibility.** Share methods and examples that others can inspect, test, and challenge.
- **Respect for rights and confidentiality.** Publish only original work or material that has been appropriately authorized for release.
- **Continuous learning.** Revise our assumptions, systems, and practices when new evidence warrants it.

## Current Status

**Early-stage initiative.** The open community and research programs are being developed. We do not claim independently verified adoption or business impact before evidence is available.
